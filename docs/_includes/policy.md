# Privacy Policy — Inkfold: PDF Reader & Signer

**Developer:** Daily Applications. **Contact:** dailyapplications@gmail.com.

## The short version
- Your documents **never leave your phone**. Inkfold opens, annotates, signs, converts and scans files on the device. There is **no account, no server and no cloud upload**, and the developer does not receive your documents or personal information.
- The app is free and shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
- There is **no analytics** and no selling of data.

## What stays on your device
To work, the app keeps a small amount of data in an encrypted database in its private storage: a reference to each document you open (the link Android provides, not the file), its name, size, page count and your last reading position; bookmarks and reading statistics; folders you linked to the library; your settings (such as language and theme); and your credit balance and counters that keep rewarded ads within their limits. The database key is generated on your phone and kept in the Android Keystore. Android backups of the app are switched off, so this data is not copied to Google Drive or a new phone. The app keeps no copies of your documents; temporary files needed while a tool runs are created in the app's private cache and deleted afterwards.

You are in control: **Private mode** (Settings → Privacy) makes the app remember nothing about the documents you open, "Clear temporary files" (Settings → Storage) removes working files, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Files you save remain where you saved them until you delete them.

## What the app does not do
It does not upload, read or analyse your documents on any server. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone or all of your files: you choose each file or folder with the Android file picker or the system "save as" screen.

## Permissions
- **Internet and network state:** to load ads and to show Google's consent form.
- **Advertising ID (AD_ID):** used by Google's advertising SDK. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
- **Biometrics:** optional app lock with your fingerprint or face. The biometric data stays in Android; the app only receives "yes" or "no".

## Ads and consent (Google AdMob)
The app displays banner ads and optional rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your region and choices, Google and its advertising partners may use identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address (and a coarse location derived from it) and device and app information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. They never receive your documents, their names or their content. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the app shows Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in **Credits → Ad privacy options**. If you decline, you can still use the app; ads may be limited, and earning credits by watching an ad may be unavailable. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Other Google services on your device
Document scanning and text recognition (OCR) use Google Play services and ML Kit. They run on your device; Google Play services may download recognition models over the network, never your documents. Reading aloud uses the speech engine installed on your phone.

## Credits
Credits are a local counter on your device used to limit some file tools. You receive a gift when you start and a small gift each day, you can earn more by choosing to watch a rewarded ad, and a developer-issued code can add credits. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought or transferred and are not tied to an account. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling resets them.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your country). Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

## How long data is kept
On your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. The developer keeps no personal data about you and cannot identify you from the app's data, because there are no accounts.

## Your rights
Because the developer does not hold your personal data, there is normally nothing to access or delete. For data processed by Google, use Google's tools (for example Ads Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com) and the privacy options inside the app. Under the GDPR you may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. Questions: dailyapplications@gmail.com.
